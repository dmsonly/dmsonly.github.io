Features

Your messages stay. The scroll goes.

DMs Only opens Instagram's own website with the distracting parts taken out, so you can talk to friends without getting pulled into a feed.

No more scrolling

No Feed. No Reels. No Explore.

They're removed completely, so there's nothing pulling you into a scroll. Search and the new post button are hidden too.

Messages

Your messages stay.

DMs, group chats, voice notes and friends' Stories still work with your existing Instagram account. Send photos, videos and voice messages like you always do.

Optional

Lock the Instagram app.

Block the real Instagram app when you're ready. You keep two 5 minute passes a day for things only the app can do, like posting a story or calling someone. Turn it off any time in Settings.

Notifications

Know when someone messages you.

Turn on unread alerts and DMs Only checks about every 15 minutes how many of your conversations are unread, then lets you know.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is DMs Only made by Instagram?

No. DMs Only is an independent app and isn't affiliated with Instagram or Meta.

Can DMs Only see my password or messages?

No. You sign in on Instagram's own page inside the app. DMs Only doesn't run its own servers, so your password, messages and login never go anywhere except Instagram.

Is it free?

Yes. There's no subscription and nothing to buy, and you don't need a new account.

Can I still use the regular Instagram app?

Yes. Locking the Instagram app is optional. If you turn it on, DMs Only uses Android's Accessibility service only to notice when Instagram opens, and you get two 5 minute passes a day to use it.

Will I still get notified about new messages?

Yes, if you turn on unread alerts in Settings. DMs Only checks about every 15 minutes and tells you how many conversations are unread.

How do I remove my data?

Go to Android Settings → Apps → DMs Only → Storage → Clear storage. This signs you out and resets the app. Your Instagram account and messages aren't affected. The privacy policy has the details.
